Real brightness is an estimate, not a measurement. It divides out both the camera's own boost and your slider. If your device does not report ISO and shutter, only the slider can be divided out and the hint under the number will say so — treat it as a floor rather than the truth. It can also read higher than current in bright scenes, when the camera is pulling exposure down rather than up. That is correct, not a bug.
